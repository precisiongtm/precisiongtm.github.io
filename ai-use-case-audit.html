Step 02
The Prioritization Workshop
Half a day with your leadership team. The session where the argument gets settled and a decision gets made.
$7,500
Half day

Your team takes the assessment before we meet. I arrive knowing what you are measured on, which of your objectives your buildable list actually serves, where the gaps are, and where your people already disagree. That pre-read is what makes a half day enough.

The format: ninety minutes on diagnosis, a thirty-minute break, then ninety minutes on decisions. The break matters. People change their minds during it.

This is not a presentation. It is a working session where your leaders argue about priorities in front of a framework, and I referee. If sales leadership is in the room, better, because adoption is where most of this dies.

  • A ranked decision, made by your team, not handed to them, tied to the objective you are actually measured on.
  • Named owners and dates for the top three workflows.
  • A business case for the first one, framed for a CFO: the metric it moves, expected impact, cost to build and run, what would prove it worked, and the kill criteria that would stop it.
  • A written readout within three business days, structured so you can forward it.
