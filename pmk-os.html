Why Teams Buy It

What changes when AI can use approved product knowledge?

AI multiplied the drafts. It did not multiply the people who know which claims are true. PMK-OS takes the routine checking off those people so their time goes to the drafts that need judgment.

01

Spend less time reviewing

Every draft gets checked against the approved source before a person reads it. Clean drafts can go straight out. Problem drafts arrive with the exact line flagged and the approved passage it breaks, so the review takes minutes.

02

Use the customer proof you already have

Sellers ask product questions in plain language and get answers built only from approved documents, with every claim cited. When the documents don't cover a question, PMK-OS says so and gives no answer. Product marketing stops fielding every objection by hand.

03

Keep up when the story changes

A new customer result, a launch or a shift in target segment shouldn't mean rewriting every prompt. Add the signal to PMK-OS. It proposes the specific edits to the approved documents. Your owner approves or dismisses each one. Approved changes reach the next answer, check or draft that pulls from the source.

How It Works

How does customer proof make it into an AI draft?

The same approved source feeds both writing and review. Here is the loop, start to finish.

  1. 1
    A draft starts from the current story. A seller or a connected AI tool writes from the approved positioning as it stands today.
  2. 2
    The team adds new evidence. Customer feedback, a win/loss note or a call excerpt goes into PMK-OS as a signal.
  3. 3
    PMK-OS proposes the change. It checks the signal against every approved document and suggests where the evidence belongs as supporting proof. A marketer reviews the edit and approves it.
  4. 4
    The same brief runs again. The new draft pulls from the updated source and uses the new proof. Nobody rewrote the prompt.
Measuring the Return

How do you measure the return from PMK-OS?

You set the baseline at kickoff. PMK-OS reports against it. Your team enters how many review requests, seller questions, collateral drafts, field inputs and messaging updates reach product marketing each week, and the minutes each one takes. PMK-OS counts only the work that actually ran through it.

Start with a single workflow you already run, such as reviewing AI-written outbound. Compare time to approval, edit rounds and claims corrected before and after. Count time saved as capacity unless spending actually falls.

How it counts

  • An answer counts only when it cites at least 1 approved source.
  • A draft that's ready to send saves a full review. A flagged draft saves half, because the problem arrives pinpointed.
  • Weekly counts are capped at your baseline, so a busy week can't inflate the total.
  • Sellers' time and the time people spend editing and approving are left out.
  • Contradictions caught are reported as risk. They are never turned into hours.

Pipeline, win rate and AI search visibility are worth watching after a pilot. PMK-OS doesn't claim them.

Common Questions

What buyers ask before a pilot.

What is PMK-OS?

PMK-OS is a product marketing operating system from Precision GTM. It keeps a company's approved positioning, product claims, product limits and customer proof in a single place. People and connected AI tools use it to check drafts before review, answer product questions with sources, and add new evidence to the story once a person approves it.

How is it different from a brand guide or an AI writer?

A brand guide is a reference people have to remember to open. An AI writer produces drafts with no record of what your company approved. PMK-OS checks drafts against the approved source, answers only from it, and proposes updates that a person approves before they count.

Can we use our existing positioning and proof?

Yes. Start with what your team already trusts: positioning, personas, competitive counters, product limits and customer evidence. Precision GTM builds the first version with your positioning owner, and your team maintains it in PMK-OS after that. Setup time depends on the state of those documents, and a public website alone is not enough to build from.

Who approves a change to the product story?

A person your company names as owner, usually in product marketing. PMK-OS proposes edits from new evidence. Nothing becomes part of the approved source until that owner approves it.

Do we have to replace our current AI tools?

No. MCP-compatible AI tools, such as Claude, connect to PMK-OS to pull approved context and check drafts. You can also audit exported call transcripts and email lists, from tools like Gong, Zoom, Teams or Clay. Bring your current tool list to the walkthrough and we'll confirm which connect today.
